Selected Projects

Mixed-Signal Spiking Neural Network Prototype

Designed a proof-of-concept SNN for MNIST recognition using LIF neurons and synapse circuits. The system is simulated in Cadence Virtuoso using a TSMC 180 nm BCD process.

Piezoelectric Energy Harvesting Circuit System

Research on low-power energy harvesting circuits, rectifier interfaces, latching mechanisms, and power transfer optimization for piezoelectric harvesters.

Digital CNN Accelerator

Developed a CNN accelerator architecture with parallel MAC units, AXI-Stream interface, BRAM-based data buffering, and integer quantization/dequantization flow.
